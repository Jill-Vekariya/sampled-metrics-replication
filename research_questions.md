# Research questions
RQ1: Do independent exact metric calculations reproduce published Table 1 to its printed precision?
RQ2: At 99 sampled negatives, do expected AP and NDCG rank A over C while full-catalogue AP and NDCG rank C over A, and is expected AUC invariant?
RQ3: Do the published rank-order changes across sample sizes appear on the predeclared grid?
RQ4 (secondary): Do these effects persist without replacement, and how does floor rank correction change the fixed-fixture bias?
These are claims about three constructed rank lists on five fixed instances, not trained models or a population of users.
